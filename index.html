What we build

Framing services built around your project

From ground-up custom homes to tight infill additions, we handle the structural framing everything else in the build depends on.

01 — Custom Homes

Custom Home Framing

Full structural framing for new custom-home construction — foundation-to-roof layout, wall and floor systems, engineered lumber, and roof trusses. We work directly from stamped plans, so every trade that follows us inherits a square, true structure.

  • Foundation-to-roof structural framing
  • Engineered lumber & truss installation
  • Shear wall & lateral bracing systems
  • Framing inspections coordinated with your GC
02 — ADUs

Accessory Dwelling Units

ADUs framed to current California code — a clean, efficient build from slab to roofline that maximizes your property's value.

03 — Remodels & Additions

Remodels & Additions

Pop-tops, second-story additions, and room extensions with clean tie-ins to your existing structure — matched framing, no shortcuts.
